About OsteoVision AI

Orthopedic Computer Vision & Morphometry

OsteoVision AI introduces a multi-angle baseline registration framework. By cross-referencing patient scans against up to six canonical projections and tracking mathematical boundary discontinuities across bone geometry, the system provides explainable, deterministic visual overlays that penetrate fracture gaps directly.
